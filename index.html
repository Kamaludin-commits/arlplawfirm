ABOUT US / THE FIRM

Legal insight.
Commercial perspective.

Ardiansyah Limbong & Partners (ALP) offers clients a wide range of integrated legal services, including commercial transactions and corporate disputes.

Our lawyers combine a practical understanding of clients’ industries and businesses with insight into the regulations that shape them. We deliver comprehensive legal advice designed to help minimise legal and business risk.

For complex transactions and contentious matters, we work with specialists and professional advisers across disciplines and jurisdictions where appropriate.

LEGAL SUPPORT / RETAINER

Consistent access
to trusted counsel.

Flexible retainer arrangements for businesses that require recurring legal support.

Typical coverage includes contract drafting and review, corporate governance, company law, regulatory updates, employment matters and intellectual property risk management.

Support is available through meetings, calls and email. Engagements may be structured on a retainer, fixed-fee, capped-fee or hourly basis.

Substantial disputes, transactions and investigations may be separately scoped. All services are subject to conflict checks, client acceptance and a written engagement agreement.
